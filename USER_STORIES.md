# User stories

## Reading a quote
**As a** visitor, **I want** to see an inspiring quote with its author when I open the page, **so that** I get motivation right away.

Acceptance criteria:
- A quote and its author are shown on page load.
- The list contains at least 20 quotes.

## Changing the quote
**As a** visitor, **I want** to press a button to get a new random quote, **so that** I can keep looking until I find one I like.

Acceptance criteria:
- Pressing "New quote" replaces the current quote.
- The same quote is not shown twice in a row.

## Copying a quote
**As a** visitor, **I want** to copy the quote with one click, **so that** I can share it in a message or a post.

Acceptance criteria:
- Pressing "Copy" puts the quote text and author in the clipboard.
- The page shows a short "Copied!" message.

## Saving favourites (optional)
**As a** visitor, **I want** to save quotes I like, **so that** I can find them again later.

Acceptance criteria:
- A quote can be added to and removed from the favourites list.
- Favourites are still there after reloading the page.

## Keeping and removing favourites
As a returning visitor, I want my saved quotes to still be there after I refresh, and to remove ones I no longer want, **so that** my list stays useful.

Acceptance criteria:
- After a reload the favourites list is unchanged.
- Pressing "Remove" deletes a quote from the list.
